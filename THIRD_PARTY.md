# Third-Party Software

KayelOS is based on Debian GNU/Linux and incorporates software and configuration
from multiple upstream projects.

Third-party components retain their original copyright notices and licenses.

Major upstream components include:

- Debian GNU/Linux
- dwm and related suckless software
- JustAGuyLinux DWM configuration/setup components
- QuickShell
- Calamares
- Penguins' Eggs
- Fastfetch
- GRUB
- Nerd Fonts
- Git and other Debian packages listed in `packages/package-manifest.txt`

The top-level GPL-3.0 license applies to KayelOS-original material where
applicable. It does not replace or override licenses attached to third-party
software or assets.

When modifying or redistributing third-party components, consult their
respective upstream licenses and copyright notices.
